About this digital study

An independent, unofficial reconstruction. Not commissioned, endorsed or sold by Jacob & Co. or Bugatti. The model is original digital geometry, not factory CAD or a 578-part manufacturing replica.

Crank phases, internal gearing, suspension displacement, hidden connections and the 60-second cage display period are engineering approximations. Sapphire uses a real-time optical approximation. Slow motion and disassembly are digital observation tools.
